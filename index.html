Urgent guidance

Get medical help now

If symptoms are severe, sudden, or worrying, contact emergency services or your maternity unit now. Do not wait for an online answer.

  • United States: call 911 for emergencies.
  • United Kingdom: call 999 for emergencies or NHS 111 for urgent advice.
  • Use your hospital maternity triage number if one was provided.
